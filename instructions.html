User Instructions
A quick guide on using ScriptShift
1 Choose Your Input Method
Select between two powerful modes:
  • Text Input: Type or paste text in any script (Arabic, Japanese, Hindi, French, etc.). Automatic Right-To-Left (RTL) formatting triggers for Arabic scripts.
  • Image OCR: Click to upload an image containing printed text to let Tesseract.js extract characters automatically(Still in Production).
2 Translate & Polish Context
Click Shift & Explore. Scripty our AI will detect the language, translate the script, and pass the output in text.
3 Deep Dive into Script History
Click Explore Language History on Wikipedia to jump directly to detailed cultural background and linguistic history for the detected script.
4 Supported Direct Scripts
Scripty includes built-in deep-linking for 12 primary world scripts:
Arabic French Spanish German Japanese Russian Hindi Chinese Italian Portuguese Korean Dutch Don't worry new scripts coming soon!!
